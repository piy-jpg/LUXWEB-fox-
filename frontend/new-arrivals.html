✦   THE FORMULATION PROCESS   ✦

BEHIND THE Innovation

Every new creation is aged and cold-extracted under zero oxygen in our Paris atelier.

01

Zero-Oxygen Cold Press

Hand-harvested botanicals from Grasse are extracted at 14°C to preserve 99.4% of raw bioactive antioxidants and enzymatic living potency.

02

Cellular Bio-Ferment

A 21-day micro-fermentation breaks down active molecules into nano-peptides that penetrate 4x deeper into the epidermal stratum corneum.

03

Every vial is bottled in authentic Miron violet glass, filtering out destructive visible light while allowing nourishing ultraviolet and infrared rays.
