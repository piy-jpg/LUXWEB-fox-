The Beginning

A Dream in a
Parisian Laboratory

Lumière was founded in 2014 by Isabelle Moreau, a biochemist and lifelong beauty obsessive who refused to believe that luxury and ethics were mutually exclusive.

Working from a small atelier in the 6th arrondissement, she spent three years developing formulations that were powerful enough to please a dermatologist, gentle enough for sensitive skin, and beautiful enough for the most discerning collector.

Today, Lumière is distributed in 42 countries, recognised by Vogue, Allure, and the British Beauty Council — but the same Parisian atelier where it all began still crafts every formula by hand.

Isabelle Moreau Founder & CEO

Our Philosophy

Clean Science

Every ingredient is rigorously screened. We never use parabens, sulfates, mineral oils, or synthetic fragrances. Our formulas are clinically proven to deliver visible results.

02

Cruelty-Free Always

Leaping Bunny certified since day one. No animal testing at any stage of production, anywhere in the world. Beauty without compromise.

Responsible Luxury

Our packaging uses 80% recycled glass and FSC-certified paper. We offset our carbon footprint and source ingredients from certified ethical suppliers.

Beauty has no single face. Our product range is designed and tested across all skin tones, types, and ages — because everyone deserves to feel luminous.

Our Journey

12 Years of Luminosity

2014

The Atelier Opens

Isabelle Moreau launches Lumière from a small Paris lab with 8 products and a big dream.

2016

Vogue Paris Feature

Our Radiance Serum is named "the only serum you'll ever need" — overnight fame and a 3-month waitlist.

2018

Global Expansion

Lumière launches in 20 countries. We open our first flagship boutique on Rue Saint-Honoré.

2020

Sustainability Pledge

100% recyclable packaging, carbon-neutral shipping, and a commitment to 0 virgin plastic by 2025.

2022

Fragrance Collection

The Lumière Fragrance House launches with 6 signature scents, earning the Fragrance Foundation Award.

2026
