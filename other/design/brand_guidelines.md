# Lumière Beauty — Brand & Design Guidelines

Design tokens, color palettes, and typographic scales defining the luxury visual identity of Lumière.

---

## 1. Color Palette

### Primary & Dark Accents
- **Noir / Deep Black**: `#0d0d0f` (Hero backgrounds, dark sections, footer)
- **Charcoal**: `#16161a` (Card backgrounds in dark mode, modal overlays)
- **Deep Slate**: `#222228` (Borders, subtle dividers)

### Luxury Metals & Highlights
- **Lumière Gold**: `#d4af37` / `#c5a059` (Brand accents, badges, buttons)
- **Champagne**: `#f4ebd0` (Muted luxury highlights, icon glow)
- **Warm Bronze**: `#9e7a44` (Hover states, subtle borders)

### Neutrals & Light Canvas
- **Ivory Cream**: `#faf8f5` (Light mode backgrounds, card containers)
- **Warm Sand**: `#f0ebe1` (Secondary backgrounds, table strips)
- **Pure White**: `#ffffff` (Card surfaces, inputs, text on dark)

---

## 2. Typography

- **Headings & Display**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Google Font, weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, Italic)
  - Editorial, graceful, Parisian haute-couture styling.
- **Body & UI**: `Outfit` (Google Font, weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700)
  - Crisp, modern, highly legible geometric sans-serif.

---

## 3. Brand Tone & Voice
- **Tone**: Understated luxury, sensorial, poetic yet scientifically grounded.
- **Imagery**: Soft studio lighting, warm specular reflections, clean product bottles, editorial portraiture.
